StarHijack

Retro 16-bit arcade shooter for iPhone

Stun enemy ships, hijack them as wingmen and blast your way through 8 stages and 8 giant bosses.

FAQ

How do I bomb? Tap the screen with a second finger. With auto-fire turned off in Settings, a BOMB button appears instead.

How do I capture an enemy? Damage it until it flashes with a cyan ring, then fly into it. It joins you as a wingman (up to two).

I bought the Pilot's License on another device. Open Settings > LICENSE and tap RESTORE.

What does the Pilot's License include? Infinite lives, every stage in stage select (including boss starts) and all future content: new pilots and new chapters.
