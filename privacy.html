Privacy Policy

StarHijack · Last updated: November 6, 2026

StarHijack is a single-player game. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your settings, high score and unlocked stages are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The Pilot's License is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple ID. The game only checks with Apple whether the license is owned.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

The game contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs. It does not ask for an account or for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The game does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
